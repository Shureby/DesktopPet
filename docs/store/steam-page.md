## English

### Short description (≤ 300 characters)

A little pet that lives on your desktop: it walks and naps on top of your windows,
and rings your alarms, to-dos and focus sessions. Remembers birthdays (lunar ones too),
steps aside for your calls and slideshows, and sends files to your phone.

### About this game

**A pet for your desktop — and a helper that never nags.**

ChimePet puts a small animal on your screen. It wanders along the bottom, hops onto the
tops of your windows, naps, chases your cursor and lets itself be picked up and thrown.
Clicks go straight through to whatever's underneath, so it never gets in the way.

And it keeps an eye on the time for you.

**Your day, delivered by a pet**
- **Alarms and timers** — once, daily, weekdays or the days you pick; snooze, and a
  quiet list of anything you missed while you were away.
- **To-dos** — type "call mom at 3pm" or "stretch in 20m"; the pet runs over and tells
  you when it's time. Repeats, all-day items, overdue reminders.
- **Focus sessions** — Pomodoro with your own lengths and work hours. The pet sits and
  works with you, then celebrates your breaks, with distinct sounds for focus and break.
- **Anniversaries** — birthdays and memorial days, including **lunar-calendar dates**
  and holidays for your region. On the day: fireworks and music, or a quiet candle.

**Knows when to step aside**
- Presenting, in a full-screen game or on a call? The pet says goodbye and comes back
  when you're done. Important alarms still get through.
- **Modes** from Lively to Quiet set how chatty it is, on a schedule if you like.

**Pet Post — files to your phone, no cloud**
- Send files, folders and text to phones and computers nearby. It works with the free
  **LocalSend** app (Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac, Linux) and with ChimePet on another
  computer. Encrypted, with icons to compare so you know it's really them.

**Yours to keep**
- No account, nothing uploaded. Your schedule stays on your computer, with automatic
  daily backups and encrypted export when you move to a new one.
- English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.

**Two pets to start**
- **Cat** — climbs screen edges and the sides of windows, pounces on your cursor.
- **Rooster** — glides down from anywhere, crows at your alarms.
- A break-time mini-game, **Safe Landing**, where each pet plays to its own abilities.

ChimePet is in **Early Access**: it's complete enough for every day, and more pets,
mini-games and features are coming — with your help.

### Tags (suggested)

Casual, Simulation, Cute, Relaxing, Cozy, Pets, Cats, Idle, Productivity (as "Utilities"

## Early Access FAQ (English)

**Why Early Access?**
ChimePet is already something we use every day: the pet, alarms, to-dos, focus sessions,
anniversaries and Pet Post all work. But a desktop pet lives with you all day, and the
best ideas for what it should do next come from the people it lives with. Early Access
lets us build the rest with you — which pets, which games, which helpers — instead of
guessing.

**Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?**
We expect around 6 to 12 months. We'll leave Early Access when the planned pets and
mini-games are in and the app has been steady for a good while.

**How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?**
Planned (subject to what players tell us):
- More pets — a dog, sheep, goat, tiger and gorilla, each with a move of its own.
- More mini-games for breaks.
- Steam Workshop: make your own pet and share it.
- Steam achievements and cloud sync of your schedule.
- A macOS version on Steam.

**What is the current state of the Early Access version?**
Fully usable every day on Windows: two pets (Cat and Rooster), alarms and timers, to-dos
with natural typing, focus sessions, anniversaries with lunar dates and regional
holidays, modes and stepping aside, Pet Post file sending (works with LocalSend), backup
and restore, the Safe Landing mini-game, in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
Expect some rough edges; tell us and we'll fix them.

**Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?**
Yes. The price will go up a little when ChimePet leaves Early Access, as more pets and
games arrive. Buying now gets you everything that comes later at no extra cost.
(Possible paid extras later, such as optional character packs, would be clearly
separate; nothing that's in the game now will be taken away.)

**How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?**
Through the Steam forums and reviews: we read them and reply. We'll post what's next and
ask what you'd like most, and regular updates will say what changed and who suggested
it.

**桌面上的宠物，也是不唠叨的小帮手。**

铃宠在你的屏幕上放了一只小动物。它会沿着屏幕底边溜达、跳到窗口顶上、打盹、追你的鼠标，也可以被拎起来扔出去。点击会直接穿过它落到下面的窗口，从不碍事。

**你的一天，由宠物送达**
- **闹钟和计时器**：一次、每天、工作日或自选日期；可以稍后提醒，你不在时错过的会悄悄列给你。

**宠物驿站：文件传到手机，不经过云端**

**数据属于你**
- 不用注册，不上传任何东西。日程只存在你的电脑上，每天自动备份；换电脑时可以加密导出。

**先有两只宠物**

铃宠正处于**抢先体验**阶段：日常使用已经足够完整，更多宠物、小游戏和功能会陆续加入，也欢迎你一起参与。

**大概会在抢先体验阶段停留多久？**
预计 6 到 12 个月。等计划中的宠物和小游戏加入、并且稳定运行一段时间后，就会结束抢先体验。

**正式版和抢先体验版计划有什么不同？**
计划中（会根据玩家反馈调整）：
- 更多宠物：狗、绵羊、山羊、老虎和大猩猩，各有自己的绝活。
- 更多休息时玩的小游戏。
- Steam 创意工坊：自己做宠物并分享。
- Steam 成就，以及日程的云同步。
- Steam 上的 macOS 版本。

**抢先体验版目前的状态如何？**
在 Windows 上可以完整地日常使用：两只宠物（猫和公鸡）、闹钟和计时器、可以直接打字添加的待办、专注时段、支持农历和地区节日的纪念日、模式和自动让开、宠物驿站文件传输（兼容 LocalSend）、备份与恢复、小游戏 Safe Landing，界面支持英文、简体和繁体中文。难免有些粗糙的地方，告诉我们，我们会修好。

**抢先体验期间和之后价格会不同吗？**
会。结束抢先体验时价格会稍微上调，因为会加入更多宠物和游戏。现在购买，之后加入的内容都不用另外付费。（以后如果有可选的付费角色包，会明确分开出售；现在已有的内容不会被拿走。）

**你们计划如何让社区参与开发？**
通过 Steam 论坛和评测：我们会阅读并回复。我们会公布接下来的计划，询问大家最想要什么；每次更新都会说明改了什么、是谁提的建议。

## Open points (to decide before pasting)

1. **Name:** ChimePet / 铃宠 / 鈴寵 assumes the rename and the trademark checks.
2. **Early Access length:** "6 to 12 months" is a placeholder.
3. **"Buying now gets you everything that comes later" vs. future character DLC:** the
   FAQ keeps both open (DLC "clearly separate"). If you'd rather promise all future pets
   free, drop the bracketed sentence.
4. **Cloud sync and Workshop** are planned, not built: they're only in the "planned"
   answer, never in "About this game".
5. **WebView2 on the Steam build:** the direct installer fetches it if missing (Tauri's
   download bootstrapper), but Steam installs the files without that installer. Windows
   11 and updated Windows 10 have it; check what the Steam build does on a PC without it
   (Steamworks can run the WebView2 installer as an install script) before launch.
